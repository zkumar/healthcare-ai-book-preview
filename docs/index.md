# Healthcare AI

*Why healthcare is the hardest domain for AI — and why that makes it the most important.*

---

This is a **preview edition** of a book in progress on artificial intelligence in healthcare: the regulatory architecture, the clinical and financial realities, and the trust every deployment must earn. The chapters here are the opening of **Part I — The Landscape**.

It is written for people entering the field — new practitioners, students, engineers crossing over from other industries — who would otherwise spend years assembling the mental model these chapters lay out directly.

## What's in the preview

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Chapter 1 — The Regulated Machine](01-the-regulated-machine/index.md)**

    Why healthcare is structurally different from every other domain AI has touched: the regulatory frameworks, the financial logic of care gaps, and the trust deficit.

- **[Chapter 2 — Anatomy of the Healthcare Enterprise](02-anatomy-of-the-healthcare-enterprise/index.md)**

    How the sectors — payer, provider, MedTech, pharma — actually fit together, and where AI lands in each.

- **[Chapter 3 — The Data Reality](03-the-data-reality/index.md)**

    The data beneath every healthcare AI system: claims, EHR, imaging, the missingness problem, and the standards that make interoperability possible.

## A living document

A book starts decaying the moment it ships — regulations shift, pathways evolve, citations go stale. This preview is built to stay current: it regenerates from the manuscript every time the source updates, so what you read here tracks the latest thinking rather than a frozen snapshot.
