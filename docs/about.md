Vijay leads Wipro's **Healthcare & Life Sciences practice** across MedTech, Payer, Provider, and Pharma. He brings **30+ years** of engineering, delivery, and consulting experience, combining deep regulatory and medical-device engineering credentials with applied expertise in **agentic AI, clinical RAG, generative-AI governance, and interoperability** — translating emerging technology into measurable clinical, operational, and commercial outcomes.

His work centers on incubating capabilities in interoperability, the Epic practice, AI-first delivery, digital surgical platforms, and connected-device ecosystems — and on advising senior executives at marquee healthcare accounts across the US, Europe, Japan, and the Middle East.

This book grew out of that practice: an attempt to crystallize knowledge gained through research, reading, implementation, and experimentation, and pass it forward to others entering healthcare AI.
